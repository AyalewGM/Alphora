# Offline virtual cash-reservation ledger (Issue #8, phase 2)

This is **purely local hypothetical paper accounting**. It is not an exchange order, broker OMS, trading engine, data feed, or authorization for Alpaca or live/paper brokerage connectivity. It does not submit, route, or receive any orders; "fills" are explicitly **synthetic receipts created by a local test or simulator**.

## Scope and invariants

- Initialize a local on-disk SQLite database with exactly **$100,000 virtual cash**, bound to a unique session ID. The original risk policy remains 0.5% planned per-trade stop risk, 10% instrument allocation, 2% session-opening-equity daily loss halt, cash-only long-only, and daily rebalancing (not yet implemented here).
- \`reserve_buy\` requires a caller-asserted reconciled \`PaperAccount\`, an existing \`BuyProposal\`, a unique idempotency key, and a pre-budgeted maximum synthetic fee. It atomically rechecks \`validate_buy\` and available cash **after accounting for all pending reservations**. Same-symbol exposure includes the larger of caller-reported marked position value and internal cost basis, plus pending reserves. Both fee budgets and full buy-limit-price notional reserve cash; the **new intent's fee budget** also counts toward the conservative 10% per-instrument allocation cap, together with earlier pending budgets and filled acquisition fees. Planned price-to-stop risk **plus the entire maximum fee budget** must fit within 0.5% of reported equity; this conservative budget does not model gap-through-stop or slippage risk.
- The single-session simulated opening-equity baseline is fixed at **$100,000**. A caller cannot change `session_open_equity` to artificially change the 2% daily-loss threshold or risk caps. The separate `equity` and `day_pnl` fields are still caller assertions; authenticated portfolio marks remain a future requirement.
- Every `reserve_buy` call, **including an idempotent retry of an existing intent**, first invokes `observe_daily_loss`, which can atomically record a one-way `halted` flag from a reconciled caller-asserted account mark or an upstream manual halt. Loss-triggered calls raise `RISK_VETO:DAILY_LOSS_HALT`; later claims of recovery do not re-enable new reservations or synthetic fills. Callers may also invoke `observe_daily_loss` explicitly without creating a new intent. These checks are **not continuous monitoring**: no price feed, quote provenance, scheduler or independent mark-to-market service is implemented. Upstream reconciliation authenticity remains unverified; a missing observer call cannot detect a loss.
- Opening an existing database and every snapshot now validate persisted intent and synthetic-fill identifier formats, symbols, and stored decimal fields, rejecting malformed records as `BOOK_STATE_CORRUPT`. These fail-closed checks also reject malformed identities and monetary strings when the database has been edited outside this module. They are **structural validations, not cryptographic authentication**.
- Opening an existing database now verifies its internal cash/receipt invariants before any additional mutation. An interrupted/failed synthetic fill rolls back as one SQLite transaction. Separate-process abrupt-exit tests now verify WAL rollback of uncommitted writes and survival of an already committed synthetic fill, including its replay idempotency. These tests simulate abrupt interpreter exit via `os._exit`, **not** OS kill during the SQLite commit, disk-write errors, filesystem corruption or power loss; they do not prove hardware-level durability.
- \`record_hypothetical_fill\` only accepts whole-share synthetic BUY fills at or below their stored limit and strictly above stored stop; quantity and fees cannot exceed pending limits or budgets. It atomically updates simulated cash, fee-inclusive acquisition cost basis (fill notional plus actual synthetic buy fee), and remaining reservation. This cost basis is used as a conservative same-symbol exposure floor on later BUY reservations, not a current market mark, tax lot, or P&L. Exact duplicate fill IDs are no-ops; conflicting duplicates are rejected.
- \`cancel_remaining\` only releases unfilled pending virtual cash and retains filled shares. A cancelled intent ID is terminal: retrying the same ID now raises `CANCELLED_INTENT_ID` instead of falsely reporting a successful new reservation. A replacement virtual buy requires a fresh intent ID; the cancelled ID remains reserved in history across restarts. \`halt\` permanently locks new reservations and new synthetic fill mutations for that session, including after restart; cancellation remains available. The database uses WAL, FULL synchronous commits and immediate write transactions. Simple corruption and missing-book metadata fail closed; this is **not cryptographic tamper evidence**.
- Accepts only a persisted file path, not \`:memory:\`, empty or SQLite \`file:\` URIs. The file must remain local and secured by the operator.

## Boundaries — must NOT be bypassed

The external \`reconciled=True\` flag and \`PaperAccount\` equity/P&L are **assertions, not independently authenticated facts**. This module does not verify market quotes, position marks, vendor/provenance timestamps, sessions, or exchange conditions. Filled holdings use **fee-inclusive acquisition cost basis** (price × shares + actual synthetic buy fees), not current market value; caller-supplied marked exposure must come from a separately reviewed reconciliation system. The 2% loss halt cannot be considered real-time market protection until portfolio marks and daily equity are independently verified. Stop prices do not guarantee stop fills or loss limits.

Sell-side intents are now implemented at the same hypothetical-receipt level: `reserve_sell` consumes recorded holdings only (held minus already-pending sell quantity), `record_sell_fill` credits proceeds net of fees at or above the stored limit within quantity and fee budgets, positions reduce at average-cost basis, and `cancel_sell` releases un-filled share reservations. The book-wide halt still blocks new sell intents and new sell fills while cancellation remains available. The cash invariant is now `cash = starting equity − buy costs + net sell proceeds`.

**Not implemented:** split/dividend/delisting adjustments, session rollover, settlement constraints, actual commissions/slippage modeling, true OMS order state, robust external tamper anchors/backups, broker reconciliation, feed-disconnect fail-safe, approvals, or automated execution. A synthetic fill is not evidence that any actual venue would fill a limit order. The full platform is **not** ready for paper connectivity, let alone real trading.

The independent phase-1 decision journal in draft PR #13 is **not** replaced or modified; eventual integration must be reviewed against event ordering, durable cross-table consistency, and replay.
