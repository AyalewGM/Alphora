# Point-in-time Catalyst Evidence Research (offline)

`alphora.research.catalysts` is an **offline source-metadata assessment**, not a news
scraper, language model, causal inference engine, portfolio signal, or trading
permission. It can run without credentials, third-party APIs, or network access.

## Contract

Call `assess_catalysts(symbol, as_of=..., previous_session_close_at=...,
events=[...])` with *verified* decision-time and prior regular-session-close
timestamps. The caller must verify trading calendars, corporate actions and
vendor feed lineage independently. Each `CatalystEvent` supplies a unique ID,
source type, full HTTPS source URL, category, direction, publisher, timestamped
published/first-seen/received metadata and a provenance attestation.

**First seen and received must be captured by a trusted collector at ingest
time, not reconstructed from a backfilled article or generated by an LLM.**
`received_at` is the point-in-time availability gate. Data arriving after
`as_of`, even when earlier published, is excluded. Events published before the
verified previous close cannot by themselves explain a new overnight gap.

Exact duplicate source IDs are deduplicated; conflicting revisions known by the
decision time require review rather than silent mutation. No version of a
conflicted event ID is emitted as eligible evidence: arrival ordering in a
caller-provided list cannot establish canonical truth. The output is identical
under any permutation of those conflicting inputs. Unknown future revisions
are excluded without poisoning earlier known evidence. Promotion/unverified sources, conflicting
positive and negative reports, invalid timestamps, or malformed records require
review. A recorded primary-source event merely establishes *potential evidence*
for a narrative, **not that it caused the gap or predicts positive returns**.

Output `PRIMARY_EVENT` means at least one primary (SEC/company) event whose
origin attestation was provided by a trusted upstream collector. This module
does not authenticate the SEC or company. `UNVERIFIED_EVENT` means only
secondary-source evidence. `NO_TIMELY_EVENT` means absence of eligible records,
not proof that no market catalyst exists. `REVIEW_REQUIRED` reflects a detected
uncertainty or contradiction.

In every case, `entry_catalyst_status` is deliberately `REVIEW_REQUIRED`:
**do not map this result to the PR #9 `VERIFIED` entry gate.** A future
independently reviewed integration must add authenticated source adapters,
event direction/novelty analysis, reliability testing, replay captures, policy
versioning and separate financial risk approval before automated paper entries.

## Running tests

`python -m unittest discover -s tests -v`

Tests use synthetic, nontradable symbols and event timestamps. They cover
post-decision arrival, old headlines, duplicate/conflicting event IDs,
contradictory directions, misleading URLs, malformed types and lack of broker
connectivity. They do not establish profitability or exchange data accuracy.
