# Research-only corporate-action fixture accounting (synthetic)

`alphora.research.corporate_actions.replay_static_position` operates on an **already-held**, long-only static share position with explicitly supplied session opening and closing timestamps, raw closing marks, and caller-attested corporate actions. This is a bounded accounting reference, **not** a strategy/backtest implementation, trade executor, paper brokerage, cash account, production portfolio ledger, or vendor verification system. There is no external connectivity.

- **2:1 split:** Shares double at the effective session's open; total basis and cash do not change. A 1:2 reverse split halves shares, retaining total basis. Resulting fractional shares fail closed because cash-in-lieu rules vary by issuer/broker and are not implemented.
- **Cash dividend:** On ex-date, the then-held whole shares earn an account receivable (recorded in equity). On the supplied payable session, the receivable becomes cash. No automatic reinvestment, withholding, foreign currency, return-of-capital tax basis adjustments, or corporate-action-derived price correction. Ex-date and payable date must be included in the supplied session window.
- **Cash-settled delisting:** Shares retire and remaining basis is separately accumulated on the terminal settlement session. The supplied *explicit* per-share terminal consideration becomes cash; zero may be explicitly attested, but unknown consideration fails. Noncash acquisition, merger/exchange, continued OTC value, bankruptcy recovery, liquidation timing, and dividends payable beyond window are **unsupported**. Never infer a zero price from a delisting or missing bar.
- **Ordering:** Each action has a unique ID, relevant effective date, announcement and version-available timestamps; conflicting actions with the same ex/effective session fail. Payments on a date are processed after its effective action. Events must be known *before* the supplied exchange open to prevent lookahead; otherwise reject. This is deliberately conservative for after-the-fact corrected event feeds.
- **Optional declared-session completeness gate:** Pass `expected_session_days=(date(...), ...)` supplied independently from the corporate-action/session input to compare *every* supplied session day, in exact order, including days with no corporate action. This rejects a silently dropped ordinary session as `SESSION_CALENDAR_MISMATCH`. Missing, duplicated, out-of-order or non-date declarations fail as `INVALID_EXPECTED_SESSION_DATES`. Without this optional parameter, the module **cannot** detect a gap that does not coincide with a recorded action. Even with matching dates, the result proves only caller-declared alignment—not an independently verified trading calendar, license, or vendor revision; retain the verified/pinned calendar externally as required by issue #20.
- **Valuation:** Only `RAW_UNADJUSTED` series are accepted. Equity = cash + shares × unadjusted closing mark + unpaid dividend receivables. Raw stock prices must reflect the actual split/ex-dividend events. No transaction fees, borrow, slippage, partial fills, financing, interest or asset selection. Daily close data must have been available before the next session open and by `as_of`.

The synthetic regression fixture models a 2:1 split, $1/share dividend ex/payable, 1:2 reverse split and known cash delisting. Its value remains $1,100 (including $100 initial cash) across five sessions, and original $600 aggregate basis is conserved through the split events and retired only on final settlement. A separately supplied 13:00 ET close is accepted, while missing/reordered sessions, late source revisions, after-open action knowledge, unknown delisting consideration and adjusted prices are rejected.

**Limits and next integration gates:** This accepts caller-declared event timestamps, calendar sessions and settlement values; it cannot authenticate a licensed vendor, true announcement availability, actual trading-session completeness, official distribution notices, price normalization, security master or delisting consideration. The independent financial reviewer must compare event ordering, valuation and payable entitlement to licensed source records and genuine exchange calendars; the QA reviewer must reproduce exact-head tests. Integrate with the draft #21 manifest / #17 pinned snapshot only after both are independently accepted; there is no automatic import into the existing #2 simple simulator. Until verified total-return accounting, an actual LEAN engine run, out-of-sample controls, and production-grade portfolio/OMS risk exist, **do not claim real-market performance or profitability**. The approved simulated $100,000 starting equity, 0.5% trade risk, 10% instrument allocation, 2% daily loss halt and daily rebalance policy are not implemented here.
